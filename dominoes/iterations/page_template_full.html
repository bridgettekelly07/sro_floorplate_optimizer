SRO dominoes

Pick a decision in Vancouver's SRO conversion process. The page traces what the published evidence says follows, year by year, with a source on every link and both sides of every disagreement. No magnitudes are asserted. No model is called.

The seven decisions on the as-is process, spaced by time. Each dashed box is one decision, placed at the stage where it is set; click it to set it. The distance between two stages follows the weeks timing.json measured between them (seven Council cases), on a log scale so a 10 week step and a 3 year step read as different lengths. A dotted gap has no case yet and is drawn at the shortest length, which says nothing about its duration. Coral on the time scale means the chosen decision acts on that step; grey text in a box means no timing data. Choosing minor repair skips Council. Coral rings show where its sourced effects land; which stage an effect sits at is the author's reading (track_map.json), not a sourced claim.

How to read it

Each row is an outcome. A bar runs from the earliest to the latest year the effect lands, counted from the decision (the vertical rule). The sign in the label column is the net direction along the chain: signs multiply, so a decrease of a decrease reads as an increase. Heavier outlines mean stronger evidence; dashed outlines mean a mechanism or theory with no grade. Rows joined by a coral bracket are a fork: two sourced claims that contradict each other, both drawn, neither chosen. A bar that runs past the horizon is clipped and ends in a pale cornflower arrow.
